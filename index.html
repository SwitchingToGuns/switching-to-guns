Switching to Guns is a single-player combat flight simcade with combined air, ground, and naval battles.

About the game

Switching to Guns combines mouse-guided flight with aircraft handling affected by speed, loadout, and damage. Choose an F-15, Light Fighter, or Carrier Fighter and equip it in the hangar before takeoff. Manage fuel and ammunition, use countermeasures against incoming missiles, and return to base to repair and rearm.

Operations range from fighter engagements to territorial ground battles and a naval invasion. Intercept enemy aircraft, support allied forces as they capture territory, and attack transports before they deliver reinforcements. Ground and naval battles continue while you fly and rearm. Outside combat, explore Crescent Bay and Harbor Reach in free flight.
